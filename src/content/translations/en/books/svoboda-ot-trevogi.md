---
title: "The Worry Cure"
author: "Robert L. Leahy"
summary: "A practical book by psychologist Robert Leahy about the nature of anxiety and how to stop letting it run your life. He explains the cognitive mechanisms behind anxious thoughts and offers exercises for dealing with uncertainty."
---

A really useful book for anxious people, with lots of techniques for everyone: for OCD, anxiety disorder and other diagnoses.

Reading it, you realise that even your experience with anxiety isn't unique: someone has already been through it before you.
