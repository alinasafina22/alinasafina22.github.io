---
title: "The Gods Always Travel Incognito"
author: "Laurent Gounelle"
summary: "Alan is about to take his own life when a stranger saves him and offers him a series of tasks that push him out of his comfort zone. A parable about fear, confidence and change."
---

I often think back to the scene where Alan goes from bakery to bakery, asks for one thing and then another, and in the end turns it all down, suffering terribly because he isn't used to being an inconvenience. I remember it whenever I catch myself wanting to be convenient for others.

A therapeutic book, but it just wasn't for me: far too much preaching and too many techniques. For me it's first and foremost a novel, not a psychology book. I wanted a stronger plot, and the ending really surprised me — I didn't quite understand it.
