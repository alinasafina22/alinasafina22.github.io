title: "The Count of Monte Cristo. Volume 1"
author: "Alexandre Dumas"
summary: "On the eve of his wedding, young sailor Edmond Dantès is falsely accused and locked up in the Château d'If. There he meets Abbé Faria, learns about a treasure and gains the knowledge that will become the basis of his future revenge."
---

For a long time I couldn't bring myself to start this book, for several reasons. First, I thought I wouldn't like an old adventure novel: I don't usually read that kind of thing. Second, it's huge, and there's no telling how long it would take to read — it feels like forever.

But after a few videos from book bloggers I gave in and started reading. Then I switched to the audiobook.

So far I've only read the first volume. Until about the middle I couldn't see where the adventure was. Out of envy, people wanted to take a poor young man's whole life away, and they managed to take fourteen years of it. I felt terribly sorry for him and for his poor father, who practically starved to death — you wouldn't wish that on your worst enemy. It didn't feel like an adventure at all; it felt like a book about the hero's suffering.

The second half, with less suffering and injustice, finally felt like an adventure. I also really enjoy watching the hero take revenge for his life and for his father.
