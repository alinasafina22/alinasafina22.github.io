---
title: "Fourth Wing"
author: "Rebecca Yarros"
summary: "Violet Sorrengail dreamed of becoming a scribe, but her mother sends her to the war college for dragon riders. Amid deadly trials she has to survive, earn a dragon and figure out the mysterious cadet Xaden."
---

One of the first fantasy books I ever read. I devoured it in a couple of days and loved the world the author built. The romance turned out to be pretty standard, nothing special.
