summary: "A shy paid companion to a wealthy lady marries the widower Maxim de Winter and comes to live at his estate, Manderley. Everything there is a reminder of Maxim’s first wife, Rebecca, who died a year earlier, and the housekeeper, Mrs Danvers, makes no secret of her dislike for the new mistress."
