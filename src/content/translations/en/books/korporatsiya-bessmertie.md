---
title: "Immortality, Inc."
author: "Robert Sheckley"
summary: "A sci-fi novel about a world where a person's mind can be moved into a new body, and death becomes a matter of technology, money and power. The hero gets pulled into a dangerous story with the corporation that controls this."
---

The book is about Thomas Blaine, who died in a car crash in 1958 and woke up in 2110: his mind had been moved into a new body. People have learned to transfer minds and technology has moved far ahead, but ordinary human vices haven't gone anywhere. The rich can live forever at the expense of the poor and the defenceless. For me the value of life lies precisely in the fact that it ends, and I don't quite understand the wish to live forever: what comes after that?

Although the novel is science fiction, it made me think about a lot of things.
