---
title: "The Secret History"
author: "Donna Tartt"
summary: "A group of classics students at an elite Vermont college live apart from everyone else under the influence of a charismatic teacher. The narrator, Richard, slowly reveals how intellectual obsession and isolation led the friends to murder."
---

Interesting plot and a lovely writing style, but for me the book dragged on far too long.

I liked the atmosphere: university life and young students who are passionate about their studies. But the events unfolded very slowly.

I definitely wouldn't reread it.
