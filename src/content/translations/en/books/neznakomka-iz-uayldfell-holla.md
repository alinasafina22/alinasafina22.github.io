---
title: "The Tenant of Wildfell Hall"
author: "Anne Brontë"
summary: "A young woman with a little son, who calls herself a widow, moves into Wildfell Hall, an old house that has stood empty for years. She keeps to herself, the neighbours gossip about her past, and Gilbert Markham, a young farmer, grows more and more drawn to her and tries to uncover her secret."
