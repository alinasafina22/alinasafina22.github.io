---
title: "What I Talk About When I Talk About Running"
author: "Haruki Murakami"
summary: "Murakami writes about his experience as a long-distance runner and connects training with writing, discipline, ageing and accepting his own limits."
---

An engagingly written book that really makes you want to run :) An unusual format for me: there's no story as such, just the author's notes on running and life. Still, it's a good read.

What I liked most is that the author started running at 33. It struck me that it's never too late to start. The book also reminded me that what matters is not so much the final result (though that counts too) as the journey itself, the process.
