---
title: "In the Woods"
author: "Tana French"
summary: "A murdered girl is found in a wood near Dublin, and detective Rob Ryan is put on the case. The crime scene is tied to his own childhood: years ago two of his friends vanished there, and he alone came back, remembering nothing."
---

An interesting mystery with a fresh plot about the Dublin Murder Squad. This is the first book in the series, and the main character here is a jerk. In the second book the main character is Rob's partner.

But I really liked the story itself, the one about the missing children.
