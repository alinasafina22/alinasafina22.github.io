---
title: "The Godfather"
author: "Mario Puzo"
summary: "The saga of the Corleones, a powerful New York mafia family. After an attempt on Don Vito's life, his son Michael, who had kept away from the family business, is gradually drawn into the struggle for power."
---

I couldn't tear myself away from this story and didn't want the book to end. On the one hand, all the characters are morally grey: they're members of the mafia. On the other hand, they too are sometimes treated unfairly.

I really liked the revenge scene: for some reason it gave me a special kind of satisfaction.
