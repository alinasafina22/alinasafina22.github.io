---
title: "Bliny"
description: "Thin Russian pancakes made with milk and boiling water."
ingredients:
  - items:
      - "3 eggs"
      - "½ tsp salt"
      - "2 tbsp sugar"
      - "1 cup flour"
      - "1 cup milk"
      - "1 cup boiling water"
      - "3 tbsp vegetable oil"
steps:
  - "Whisk the eggs with the sugar and salt."
  - "Add half of the flour, then the milk, the rest of the flour and the boiling water. Keep whisking the whole time so there are no lumps."
  - "Finally pour the vegetable oil into the batter and stir."
  - "Fry on both sides in a hot pan."
---
